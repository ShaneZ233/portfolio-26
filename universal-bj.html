Full Experience Booking

A new feature for tourists to easily purchase all their desired experiences in one place.

Overview

Universal Beijing Resort is a premier entertainment destination offering immersive rides, dining, and shopping experiences. The official app supports trip planning, ticket purchases, wait times, personalized itineraries, and park navigation for a smoother guest experience.

Problem Statement

The app had two separate shopping entry points with different product selections. Single items and bundles were mixed together, creating cognitive load and making it harder for visitors to decide what to buy.

😵‍💫

Painpoint 1

Disconnected product channels confuse users. They do not know where to browse.

😓

Painpoint 2

Messy page hierarchy frustrates users. They cannot find what they need.

🤔

Painpoint 3

Unclear detail pages overwhelm users. They struggle to make decisions.

😤

Painpoint 4

Redundant form filling annoys visitors. It slows them down during purchase.

Impact

Reduced 15%

Task efficiency improvement through streamlined purchase page structure.

Increased 25%

App engagement after introducing the end-to-end booking feature.

Boosted 12%

Average order value by optimizing the user experience.

Ideation Process

Solution1

Shopping Cart, One-Time Checkout

The shopping cart model made bulk purchases easier but didn’t suit tickets and express passes, where prices and availability change quickly. Items could expire, and it slowed decision-making.

Solution2

Add-on, Personalize Your Order

The add-on model enables users to purchase extras in one transaction, speeding up checkout. However, for items requiring confirmation, frequent page switches can disrupt the flow and cause confusion.

Iterated Solution

Progress Indicator

Guiding users with visual steps while reducing page transitions and enhancing immersion.

Integrate brand personality into UI design

Takeaways

  • Insights are more important than feedback. The value of user interviews is understanding the essence of the problem, not simply executing every request.
  • Consider extreme situations as well as the ideal path. Edge scenarios reduce frustration when the real product environment becomes messy.
  • Design is a continuous process of trade-offs and iteration. Real-world constraints require the most suitable solution for the current scenario.
